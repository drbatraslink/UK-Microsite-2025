Alopecia Areata is an autoimmune condition causing sudden, patchy hair loss. It can affect anyone and may involve the scalp, face, or body.

Triggers include stress, genetics, immune system issues, and environmental factors. Understanding these helps create a personalised treatment plan.

What is Androgenetic Alopecia (AGA)?

AGA, commonly known as male or female pattern baldness, is a hereditary condition causing gradual hair thinning. It affects many people in The UK and typically worsens with age if left untreated.

What causes AGA in men and women?

AGA is primarily driven by genetics and hormonal changes. Other factors like stress, diet, and lifestyle can also contribute to the severity of hair loss.

Can AGA be treated effectively?

Yes, early diagnosis and personalised treatment can slow hair loss and promote regrowth. Options include homeopathy combined with advanced treatments tailored to individual needs.

How long does AGA treatment usually take?

Visible improvement often occurs within 3 to 6 months, but treatment duration varies. Consistency and regular follow-ups help achieve the best results.

Are AGA treatments safe for all ages?

Most treatments are safe for adults. A thorough consultation ensures personalised care suited to your health profile and hair loss stage.

When should I consult a specialist for AGA?

If you notice gradual thinning, widening partings, or a receding hairline, early consultation helps diagnose and manage AGA before it progresses further.

What is psoriasis and how common is it in The UK?

Psoriasis is a chronic skin condition causing red, scaly patches. It affects around 2% of The UK population and can vary in severity from mild to severe.

What triggers psoriasis flare-ups?

Common triggers include stress, infections, cold weather, certain medications, and lifestyle factors. Identifying and managing triggers is key to controlling symptoms.

Can psoriasis be treated naturally?

Yes, natural treatments like homeopathy can help reduce inflammation and manage symptoms safely, alongside conventional care, for long-term relief.

How quickly can I expect results from psoriasis treatment?

Improvement timelines vary, but many patients notice relief within a few months of consistent treatment and lifestyle adjustments.

Are psoriasis treatments safe for sensitive skin?

When should I seek medical advice for psoriasis?

What is vitiligo?

Vitiligo is a condition that causes white patches on the skin due to pigment loss. It affects around 1% of people in The UK. While it’s not contagious or painful, it can impact confidence and requires early treatment for better management.

What causes vitiligo?

Vitiligo is believed to be autoimmune, where the body attacks pigment cells. Genetics, environmental factors, and stress can contribute. Understanding triggers helps tailor personalised treatment plans for better repigmentation outcomes.

Can vitiligo be treated effectively?

Though there’s no permanent cure, treatments like homeopathy can help restore skin pigmentation and manage symptoms safely. Consistent care improves chances of repigmentation and helps control spread.

How long does vitiligo treatment take?

Treatment duration varies widely depending on individual response and severity. Some patients begin to see repigmentation within several months, but long-term care is often needed for best results.

Is vitiligo treatment safe for all skin types?

Yes. Treatments are personalised, gentle, and suitable for all skin types.

When should I seek treatment for vitiligo?

Early consultation is important if you notice white patches spreading or new areas forming. Prompt intervention can improve management and slow progression.

What is eczema?

Eczema is a common skin condition causing dry, itchy, red, and inflamed patches. It affects people of all ages and can flare up due to triggers like allergens, weather, or stress. Effective management often requires personalised treatment and lifestyle adjustments.

What causes eczema flare-ups?

Eczema flare-ups are often triggered by allergens, irritants, changes in weather, stress, or genetics. Identifying and avoiding personal triggers is essential to reduce symptoms and prevent frequent flare-ups.

Can eczema be treated naturally?

Yes, natural treatments like homeopathy offer safe, personalised care by addressing eczema’s root causes. These treatments help reduce inflammation, itching, and dryness without relying on any harsh chemicals.

How soon will eczema improve with treatment?

The timeline varies, but many people see noticeable improvements within weeks to months. Consistent treatment and avoiding triggers are key to controlling symptoms and achieving long-term relief.

Are eczema treatments safe for sensitive skin?

When should I seek medical advice for eczema?

What is PCOS/PCOD?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) and Polycystic Ovary Disease (PCOD) are hormonal disorders affecting women of reproductive age. They can cause irregular periods, weight gain, acne, hair thinning, and fertility challenges.

What causes PCOS/PCOD?

PCOS/PCOD is mainly caused by hormonal imbalances, genetics, and insulin resistance. Lifestyle factors like stress and diet can also influence symptoms and severity.

How is PCOS/PCOD diagnosed?

Diagnosis typically involves a combination of medical history, physical examination, blood tests for hormone levels, and ultrasound scans to check ovarian cysts.

Can PCOS/PCOD be managed naturally?

Yes. Natural treatments, including homeopathy and lifestyle changes, can help regulate hormones, improve symptoms, and support fertility safely without any side effects.

What lifestyle changes help manage PCOS/PCOD?

A balanced diet, regular exercise, stress management, and adequate sleep can significantly improve symptoms and overall hormonal balance.

When should I seek medical advice for PCOS/PCOD?

If you experience irregular periods, unexplained weight gain, excessive hair growth, or difficulty conceiving, consult a healthcare professional for diagnosis and personalised care.

What causes acne?

Acne develops due to excess oil production, clogged pores, bacteria, and inflammation. Hormonal changes, stress, diet, and genetics can also trigger or worsen breakouts.

Can acne be treated naturally?

Yes, natural treatments like homeopathy can address underlying causes such as hormonal imbalance and inflammation, offering safe, personalised care.

How long does acne treatment take?

Improvement timelines vary, but many patients see noticeable changes within 8 to 12 weeks. Consistency and early intervention improve outcomes.

Are acne treatments safe for sensitive skin?

Most natural acne treatments are gentle and suitable for sensitive skin, avoiding irritants and side effects associated with many conventional treatments.

Can diet affect acne?

Diet can influence acne severity. Foods high in sugar and dairy may worsen symptoms, while a balanced diet rich in vitamins supports skin health.

When should I see a dermatologist?

Consult a specialist if acne is severe, persistent, or causing scarring. Early professional care can prevent worsening and provide effective treatment plans.
